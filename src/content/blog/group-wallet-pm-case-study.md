---
title: "Group Wallet: Pooling trip money before anyone spends it"
tag: "Group Wallet · Product concept"
excerpt: "A concept for collecting trip contributions up front and making shared spending a unanimous decision."
---

On a group trip, one person often pays and then has to collect money from everyone else. My concept moves that coordination earlier: everyone contributes to a shared pool before the trip.

When someone wants to spend from the pool, the group reviews the request and approves it unanimously. I worked through a north-star metric and six supporting metrics for the concept.

I also considered regulatory constraints and what happens when someone doesn’t approve a request in time. Group Wallet is a product concept, not a launched payment product.
